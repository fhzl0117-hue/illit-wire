SEOUL — SEPTEMBER 2026

A Look Neither
One Will Start.

ILLIT's fourth EP takes its name from mamihlapinatapai — a word for a look shared by two people who each want the other to make the first move. Released April 30, 2026, the five-track EP opened at #2 on Korea's Circle chart, #3 on Billboard Japan's Hot Albums, and #1 on Billboard's US World Albums — the group's first Platinum certification at home. Lead track "It's Me" landed a mixed 7.25/10 from The Bias List, praised for its production but knocked as "meme music" built for short clips over songwriting. The Press Start♥ tour has since run through Japan, Hong Kong, and KCON LA, and now heads to THE FACT MUSIC AWARDS in Busan on September 19 before encore dates in Incheon and Tokyo Bay. This page is a working build of how ILLIT Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking GLLIT — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on the lineup: ILLIT is an official five-member group — Yunah, Minju, Moka, Wonhee, and Iroha, all finalists on Mnet's R U Next? survival show. Moka has paused group activities twice in 2026 to focus on her health, most recently in June, and performed offstage — including at KCON LA in August — as the group continued as four. Belift Lab has not announced any change to her membership status or a return date, so this page treats her as a member on hiatus, not as a departure, and won't say otherwise without a dedicated, corroborated source.

SEOUL — APR 30

Mamihlapinatapai lands ILLIT's first US World Albums #1

ILLIT's fourth EP debuted at #2 on Korea's Circle Albums chart and #3 on Billboard Japan's Hot Albums, while topping Billboard's US World Albums chart and cracking the Billboard 200 at #26. It also earned the group's first Platinum certification from the Korea Music Content Association, for shipments past 250,000 units.

7.25 / 10THE BIAS LIST
"It's Me is meme music... this works for quick engagement in shortform content, but tends to result in a thin discography that struggles to stand the test of time."

Mamihlapinatapai (2026): ILLIT's fourth EP, and its most chart-successful yet. The Bias List's review of lead track "It's Me" landed at a mixed 7.25/10 (Grade C) — the production beat scored an 8, but the review argued the song leans on just three core ideas across its 2:19 runtime, following a "talking over a techno beat" formula the reviewer sees as prioritizing viral, algorithm-friendly moments over complete songwriting.

It's a useful reminder that commercial success and critical reception don't always move together — the EP became the group's best-charting release internationally even as this particular review pushed back on the single that led it.
